What this is, and isn't

Every figure comes from Pinellas County Property Appraiser public records, refreshed monthly. It is not a listing site: there are no asking prices, and the county's value for a home often runs below what it sells for. Treat the numbers as a starting point for questions to your agent, lender and insurer, not as advice.
