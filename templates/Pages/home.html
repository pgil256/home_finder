Your tax bill, not the seller's

A long-time owner's taxes are capped. Yours reset when you buy. Each home shows an estimate for a new owner, with and without the homestead exemption, and a calculator for the monthly payment and cash to close.

What to check before you offer

FEMA flood zone, evacuation zone, the last roof permit, seawalls, and recorded sinkhole activity or contamination, from county and FEMA records for that parcel.

What this is, and isn't

The figures come from Pinellas County Property Appraiser public records, refreshed monthly, and FEMA's flood data. It is not a listing site: there are no asking prices, and the county's value for a home often runs below what it sells for. Treat the numbers as a starting point for questions to your agent, lender and insurer, not as advice.
