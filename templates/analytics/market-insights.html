How these are picked

Valued far above the rest uses the interquartile range (IQR): take the middle half of values, measure how wide it is, and flag anything more than one and a half times that width above it. It is a standard way to find values that sit apart from the pack.

Taxed on far less than the value lists the biggest gaps between the county's value and the assessed value the owner pays tax on. A long-time owner's Save Our Homes cap is the usual reason, and that cap does not pass to a buyer.

Highest tax for the value ranks the county's tax figure before exemptions as a share of value.
